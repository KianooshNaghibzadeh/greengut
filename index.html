Your samples

Read your flock's qPCR results against healthy chicks.

Upload the Ct values your lab returns for 20 gut bacteria. GreenGut turns them into estimated shares of total bacteria, places each one among – healthy chicks aged 0 to 21 days, and builds a three-page report card for you or your vet.

What to send

One CSV, one row per sample, saved from Excel. Start from the blank form so the column names match. The example values below come from our example file.

ColumnExampleWhat goes in it
Sample_IDYour own label for the sample
Age_DaysAge of the flock in days. The comparison covers 0 to 21 days.
Total_Bacteria_Ct_1, _2The total-bacteria (16S) assay in duplicate. Every other bacterium is expressed against it.
Lactobacillus_Ct_1, _2One duplicate pair for each of the 20 bacteria. Replicates more than 0.5 cycles apart are flagged.
Lactobacillus_Efficiency_pctAssay efficiency. 90 to 110 is expected; 100 is assumed if left empty.
Salmonella_Ct_1Write ND, <LOD or <LOQ exactly as the lab reports it. These are never counted as zero.
Negative_Control_Ct, Extraction_Control_CtAmplification before cycle 35 in either blank is reported as possible contamination.
Probiotic, Antibiotic, FCROptional flock details. They are printed on the card beside the result.

What comes back

A three-page report card for each sample, saved as a PDF. Your file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

Page 1

Score and verdict

A gut balance score from 0 to 100, where it falls among healthy chicks, a dysbiosis risk level and a suggested next step.

Page 2

Every bacterium

Each amount drawn against the healthy-chick range, with its percentile and a status: in range, keep an eye on it, or needs attention.

Page 3

Flags and lab checks

What each flagged bacterium usually points to, panel diversity, similarity to healthy chicks, and the replicate and control checks.

Want to see one first? The example file holds three flocks: one in good shape, one to watch and one that needs attention.
